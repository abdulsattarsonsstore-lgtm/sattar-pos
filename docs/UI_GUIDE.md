# Abdul Sattar Sons POS - UI Guide

## Design Philosophy

The interface should be:

- Simple
- Fast
- Professional
- Easy for shop staff to use
- Desktop-first
- Mobile responsive

---

# Colour Palette

## Primary

Blue

Purpose:
Buttons, active menu items, links.

---

## Secondary

White

Purpose:
Backgrounds and cards.

---

## Accent

Orange

Purpose:
Alerts, notifications, highlights.

---

## Success

Green

Purpose:
Successful sales, stock available.

---

## Warning

Yellow

Purpose:
Low stock.

---

## Danger

Red

Purpose:
Delete actions, out of stock, errors.

---

# Typography

Headings

Bold

Body

Readable

Numbers

Monospaced where useful (prices, stock)
